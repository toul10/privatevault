# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and discrimination are not tolerated — and nobody may pressure anyone to share secrets, vault
files or Recovery Kits, for any reason.

**Reporting.** Use GitHub's *Report content* option on the issue or comment concerned. Reports are reviewed
confidentially by the maintainer (GitHub user `toul10`), who may edit or remove content and block participants who
break this code.

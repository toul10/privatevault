# Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · applies to PrivateVault for macOS, version 1.x

PrivateVault is built so that its author never receives your data.

- **No data collection.** The app collects no personal data, usage data, analytics, crash reports or device
  identifiers.
- **No network access.** The app has no network entitlement, so the macOS App Sandbox blocks every connection. It
  does not sync, check for updates or contact any server — including the author's.
- **Your vault stays on your Mac.** Your items are encrypted and stored only in the app's sandbox container on your
  Mac. The Secret Key is kept in your macOS Keychain, protected by the Secure Enclave where available.
- **Backups go only where you put them.** Encrypted `.privatevault` backups are written only to locations you
  choose. If you choose a cloud-synced folder, that provider stores the encrypted file under its own terms.
- **Touch ID** is handled by macOS and the Secure Enclave; the app never receives fingerprint data.
- **No recovery by the author.** Because nothing is ever sent to us, we cannot see, recover or reset your data or
  your Master Password.

**Purchases and support.** If you buy PrivateVault or contact support, the store or payment provider you use
processes the data needed for that transaction under its own privacy policy. That data is never connected to your
vault, which never leaves your Mac.

**Questions:** <https://github.com/toul10/privatevault/issues> — never post secrets.

Changes to this policy are published in this file with a new effective date.
